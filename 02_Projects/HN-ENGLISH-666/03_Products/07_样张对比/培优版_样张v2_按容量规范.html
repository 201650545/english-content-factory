2 / 5
borrow / lend / keep
borrow借入 from sb.
lend借出 to sb.
borrow / lend 是瞬间动作，不能接「一段时间」→ 只能用 keep
stop doing / stop to do
stop doing停止正在做
stop to do停下来去做
doing 指向「已经在做」，to do 指向「接下来要做」
say / speak / talk / tell
say接内容　speak接语言
talk后加介词　tell后接人
四个词都能译「说」，考的是后面跟什么
arrive / reach / get to
arrive at/in小地点用 at、大地点用 in
reach及物，直接接宾语
reach 后不加介词；get 后必须加 to（get home 除外）
make / do
make制作出、使……变得
do做（活动、作业）
do one's homework / do the housework；make a decision / make progress
look / see / watch / read
look at看（动作，加 at）
see看见（结果）
watch 用于动态（球赛、电视）；read 用于文字书籍
hear / listen to
hear听见（结果，及物）
listen to听（动作，加 to）
hear from = 收到某人来信；hear of = 听说过
bring / take
bring带来（朝说话人方向）
take带走（离开说话人）
方向相反：Bring it here. / Take it away.
because / because of
because+ 句子
because of+ 名词 / 短语
because of the rain = because it rained
hard / hardly
hard努力地；猛烈地（adv.）
hardly几乎不（否定）
hardly 不是 hard 的副词形式，意思完全变了

① 先看示范2 道已完成的例题，看清「词义线索」和「词形线索」
示范 1My father always tells me to be careful with money. 　词义线索：be ___ with → 固定搭配 be careful with
示范 2Last month I spent all my pocket money on a new game. 　词形线索：Last month → 过去时，spend 要变 spent
小结示范两题分别示范了「靠固定搭配定词」和「靠时间状语定形」两条路子。

4 / 5
01 enjoy 的接格
✗ 错She enjoys to read books.
✓ 对She enjoys reading books.
直接记：enjoy + doing
练：I enjoy ______ (draw) pictures.
02 look forward to 的 to
✗ 错I'm looking forward to see you.
✓ 对I'm looking forward to seeing you.
直接记：这里的 to 是介词，后面接 doing
练：I'm used to ______ (get) up early.
03 瞬间动词 + 一段时间
✗ 错He has borrowed the book for two weeks.
✓ 对He has kept the book for two weeks.
直接记：for + 时间段 → 必须换成延续性动词
练：I have ______ (have) this bike for three years.
04 make sb. do 的被动
✗ 错I was made wait for an hour.
✓ 对I was made to wait for an hour.
直接记：主动省 to，被动还原 to
练：The boy was made ______ (clean) the room.
05 不可数名词
✗ 错He gave me an information.
✓ 对He gave me a piece of information.
直接记：information / news / advice 都不可数
练：Here is a piece of ______ (news) for you.
06 宾语从句语序
✗ 错Do you know where is he?
✓ 对Do you know where he is?
直接记：连接词 + 主语 + 谓语（陈述语序）
练：Could you tell me when ______ (the train / leave)?
07 主谓一致
✗ 错One of the boys are from Hunan.
✓ 对One of the boys is from Hunan.
直接记：one of + 复数名词，谓语跟 one 走（单数）
练：One of my friends ______ (like) swimming.
08 感叹句
✗ 错What beautiful the flowers are!
✓ 对How beautiful the flowers are!
直接记：How + 形容词；What + 名词短语
练：______ a clever boy he is!

① 只接 doing
  • enjoy doing喜欢做enjoy oneself 玩得开心（oneself 不能换成 yourself 之外的）
  • finish doing做完finish with sth. 用完某物
  • avoid doing避免做后面也可接名词，但绝不能接 to do
  • mind doing介意做Would you mind doing…? 答 Not at all 才是不介意
  • be worth doing值得做be worth doing sth. 值得做某事，worth 后不接 to do
② 只接 to do
  • decide to do决定做名词形式 decision：make a decision
  • plan to do计划做make a plan for… 为……制定计划
  • hope to do希望做没有 hope sb. to do 这种说法，别照 wish 套
  • refuse to do拒绝做refuse 后接 to do，不接 doing
  • would like to do想要做would like sb. to do 想要某人做
动词后面接什么？
③ 两者都可，意思不同
  • stop doing停止正在做 / stop to do停下来去做He stopped smoking. 戒烟 / He stopped to smoke. 停下来去抽
  • remember doing记得做过 / to do记得要做doing 指向已发生，to do 指向未发生
  • forget doing忘了做过 / to do忘了要做与 remember 同一套判断法则
  • try doing试着做（换办法） / try to do努力做try one's best to do 尽最大努力做
  • go on doing继续同一件事 / go on to do接着做另一件事与 stop 同一套判断法则
判断法则：doing 指向「已发生」，to do 指向「未发生」
④ 动词 + 宾语 + 动词
  • want sb. to do想要某人做want to do 想做（主语自己）
  • ask sb. to do要求某人做否定形式 ask sb. not to do
  • let sb. do让某人做let 后不加 to，也不用于被动
  • make sb. do使某人做被动要还原：be made to do
  • tell sb. to do告诉某人做否定形式 tell sb. not to do
注意：主动语态 make sb. do 不加 to；被动才还原为 be made to do
